# The Forge Philosophy

> Great stories are created by Game Masters and players.

> Forge exists to help make those stories more vivid.

The Forge modules are not designed to replace the Game Master.

They are designed to support the Game Master.

Every module removes small amounts of repetitive work or provides carefully crafted inspiration, allowing the GM to focus on what matters most:

Creating memorable moments around the table.


Rules determine what happens.

Storytelling determines how it feels.

Forge modules intentionally focus on the second part.


Forge modules never decide the story.

They provide building blocks.

The Game Master decides how those pieces become a memorable scene.


Every design decision follows one simple rule:

Never take creative control away from the Game Master.
